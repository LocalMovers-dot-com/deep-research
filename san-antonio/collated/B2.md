# Regulatory & Permitting Framework — San Antonio Collated Research (2026)

## Overview
This prompt researched the complete regulatory and permitting stack governing manufactured home transport and installation in the San Antonio metro, covering TDHCA licensing, TxDMV/TxDOT oversize permits, DPS/escort requirements, Bexar County rules, City of San Antonio zoning, and adjacent city regulations.

### Gemini Summary
- Most exhaustive treatment of TDHCA licensing requirements including education costs ($150 Core, $50 Installer), exam fees ($85 Core, $70 Installer via PSI), and provisional license mechanics
- Detailed the TDHCA license fee table across all categories (Manufacturer $850, Retailer $550, Broker $350, Installer $350) with expired-license penalty tiers
- Provided the most granular TxDOT highway maintenance fee schedule (80k-120k: $150, 120k-160k: $225, etc.) and super-heavy load vehicle supervision fee ($35)
- Covered SA metro curfews: Loop 1604 prohibited 7-9 AM and 4-6 PM inside the loop, IH-35/Loop 1604 weekend blackout (Fri 9 PM to Mon 5 AM)
- Detailed Bandera/Kerr County restrictions including SH-16 height limits and signal contractor requirements

### Claude Summary
- Only platform to cover interstate permitting comprehensively (Louisiana $10, New Mexico $25, Oklahoma $40, Mexico border crossings)
- Provided the most precise statutory citations: Texas Transportation Code Ch. 623 Subchapter E, TAC Title 10 Part 1 Ch. 80
- Clarified that DPS does NOT operate a formal escort program -- escorts are private civilian pilot cars, not law enforcement
- Most detailed local jurisdiction coverage: San Antonio ($78 permit, MH/MHC/MHP zoning districts), Bexar County ($750 flat, voluntary permits in unincorporated areas), New Braunfels (B-1 zones only), Boerne, Seguin, Kyle, San Marcos, Schertz
- Covered floodplain requirements including UDC Appendix F (BFE + 1 foot), and the 50% substantial improvement rule

### ChatGPT Summary
- Most methodical source-citation approach with explicit "accessed date" and "publication date" tracking for every claim
- Focused on the TxDMV/TxPROS system mechanics and operating authority verification (USDOT number, FMCSA MCMIS checks)
- Distinguished clearly between TxDMV (permits) vs. TxDOT (route restrictions) jurisdictional split, noting the January 1, 2012 transfer
- Provided detailed TDHCA installation acceptance pipeline: Form T timing, provisional installer inspection requirements (5 installations without violations for full license)
- Covered San Antonio District restriction document specifics but noted access limitations on certain detailed provisions

## Areas of Agreement
- All three platforms agree on core permit fees: $40 single-trip, $1,500 annual, $60 general single-trip
- All agree on TDHCA Form T fees ($75 + $25/section) and 7-day filing deadline (3 days for provisional)
- All agree on the 6 national holiday transport blackouts
- All agree TxPROS is the routing/permitting system and that carriers must follow exact permitted routes
- All agree the TDHCA installer license is $350 biennial with mandatory education requirements
- All agree that dimensional limits trigger route inspection certifications (20' wide, 18'11" high, 125' long)

## Areas of Divergence
- Escort thresholds: Gemini says escorts required at 14' width; Claude and ChatGPT say no escort needed up to 16' width -- Claude and ChatGPT cite the specific MH-only escort rules under Transportation Code 623.099, which differ from general oversize rules
- DPS involvement: Gemini describes off-duty officers at $45-$50/hr; Claude explicitly states DPS does not run a formal escort program; ChatGPT notes law enforcement "may be required" per route/conditions
- SA installation permit: Gemini cites $141.75 (FY23-24); Claude cites $78 (FY2025 revised Oct 2025) -- likely reflects a fee schedule update
- Only Claude covered interstate permitting and border crossings in detail
- Only Gemini provided the full TDHCA license fee table with expired-license penalties

## Recommended Data for LocalMovers.com
- **Escort rules**: Use Claude's MH-specific thresholds (no escort up to 16', 1 escort 16'-18', 2 escorts over 18') as they cite the specific manufactured housing statute rather than general oversize rules
- **Permit costs**: Use consensus $40/section single-trip with the ChatGPT-noted ~$41.16 effective cost including processing surcharge
- **SA installation permit**: Use Claude's $78 figure as it cites the most recent fee schedule (FY2025, revised Oct 2025)
- **Consumer verification**: Direct homeowners to TDHCA's online License View portal (mhweb.tdhca.state.tx.us) to verify installer credentials -- all platforms reference this
- **Zoning warning**: Emphasize that SA limits MH to three special zoning districts (MH, MHC, MHP) per Claude's analysis of the UDC
- **Interstate moves**: Reference Claude's neighboring-state permit costs for any cross-border content (LA $10, NM $25, OK $40)
- **Loop 410 rule**: Highlight that no oversize loads can transit through Loop 410 unless origin/destination is within the loop -- this is the #1 SA-specific routing constraint
