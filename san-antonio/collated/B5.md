### Gemini Summary
- Provided the most detailed mechanical/equipment narrative: toter trucks, hydraulic jacking, marriage-line splitting, bulldozer requirements for Hill Country terrain
- Profiled fewer companies but in greater operational depth, including Martinez Mobile Homes' bulldozer capability and Texas Elite's FMCSA safety concerns (50% driver OOS rate)
- Three-tier pricing model: Budget (transport-only $1,000-$3,500 SW), Standard (full-service $3,000-$8,000 SW / $7,000-$15,000 DW), Premium ($15,000-$25,000+ complex placements)
- Emphasized geographic stratification: flat Bexar/Guadalupe counties = standard tier; Hill Country Kendall/Bandera = premium tier requiring tracked bulldozers
- Discussed "exclusionary bidding" where premium operators intentionally price high on dangerous/undesirable jobs

### Claude Summary
- Most comprehensive company profiling: 8 named operators plus Bennett (national), each with USDOT numbers, fleet sizes, BBB status, phone numbers, and pricing tier assignments
- Identified Alamo Transport as the dominant local player (18 power units) -- not profiled by the other two platforms
- Provided the sharpest consumer verification guidance: FMCSA SAFER for safety records, TDHCA License View portal for installer credentials, BBB for complaint history
- Detailed the three-tier market with consumer-actionable price ranges and explicit "who operates here" for each tier
- Noted SB 785 (effective Sept 1, 2026) will expand placement zones, likely boosting transport demand

### ChatGPT Summary
- Most cautious profiling approach, limited to companies verifiable through FMCSA SAFER and BBB only (excluded Yelp/Google due to access limitations)
- Flagged a critical consumer warning on Martinez Mobile Homes: BBB profile shows an F rating and a TxDMV common carrier license revocation alert (Jan 31, 2025) for failure to maintain insurance -- contrasts sharply with Claude's A+ BBB rating for the same company
- Provided a structured consumer verification checklist: TDHCA license check, FMCSA SAFER snapshot, BBB complaint review, insurance verification
- Noted that Affordable Space Mobile (USDOT 576322) shows as INACTIVE in FMCSA -- a red flag for consumers
- Emphasized that interstate moves thin the competitive field dramatically because most local operators are intrastate-only

## Areas of Agreement
- All three platforms characterize the market as highly fragmented with predominantly small (1-5 truck) operators
- All identify RH Mobile Home Installers as a solid A+ BBB-rated mid-tier operator with 4 power units
- All agree Texas Elite MH Transport has strong BBB ratings but operates just 1 power unit
- All agree the three-tier model (budget/standard/premium) accurately describes the market, with budget = transport-only and premium = complex/long-distance
- All emphasize consumer due diligence is critical given the prevalence of small, minimally-regulated operators

## Areas of Divergence
- Martinez Mobile Homes: Claude says A+ BBB accredited (43 years); ChatGPT says F rating with TxDMV license revocation alert -- this is a significant factual discrepancy that requires verification (BBB ratings can change; the Jan 2025 revocation may have triggered a downgrade)
- Company coverage breadth: Claude profiled 8+ operators including the national carrier Bennett; Gemini focused on ~5; ChatGPT covered 6 with the most conservative sourcing
- Alamo Transport (18 units) was only profiled by Claude -- the other platforms may not have discovered it
- Pricing bands overlap but diverge at the edges: Gemini's standard SW tops at $8,000; Claude's at $8,000; ChatGPT's at $9,000

## Recommended Data for LocalMovers.com
- **Lead with the three-tier framework**: Budget (transport-only, $1,000-$3,500 SW), Standard (full-service, $5,000-$8,000 SW / $10,000-$18,000 DW), Premium ($15,000-$30,000+ complex)
- **Company profiles**: Use Claude's comprehensive list as the baseline, but cross-check Martinez Mobile Homes' current BBB status given ChatGPT's contradictory F-rating finding
- **Consumer verification checklist**: (1) TDHCA License View portal for installer credentials, (2) FMCSA SAFER for USDOT status and safety record, (3) BBB for complaint history, (4) Verify current insurance before signing any contract
- **Key market fact**: Alamo Transport is the only large local fleet (18 units); all other operators run 1-6 trucks -- this means peak-season capacity constraints are real
- **Hill Country warning**: Moves into Kendall, Bandera, Kerr counties require operators with specialized equipment (bulldozers, tracked vehicles); standard flat-terrain operators cannot serve these areas
- **Interstate limitation**: Most SA operators are intrastate-only (not authorized for interstate) -- consumers moving out of Texas need national carriers like Bennett Truck Transport
