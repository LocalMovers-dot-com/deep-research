# Cost Breakdown by Home Type — San Antonio Collated Research (2026)

## Overview
This prompt researched the complete cost breakdown for moving single-wide, double-wide, and triple-wide manufactured homes in the San Antonio metro area, including transport-only vs. full-service pricing, line-item costs, permits, utility hookups, and all-in realistic totals.

### Gemini Summary
- Provided the most exhaustive single-document analysis with extremely granular line-item breakdowns and per-mile blended rate calculations
- Calculated all-in realistic averages: SW $12,450, DW $19,400, TW $29,500 -- the highest estimates among the three platforms
- Detailed the regulatory architecture including TxDOT, TDHCA, and municipal permitting with specific fee amounts ($141.75 SA installation permit, New Braunfels $0.50/sqft permit)
- Profiled specific carriers by name with FMCSA safety data (Texas Elite at 50% driver OOS rate vs. 6.67% national average)
- Emphasized that transport cost is a fraction of true all-in cost, with fixed setup costs dominating the financial picture

### Claude Summary
- Provided the most structured data tables with clear transport-only vs. full-service splits and median/mean calculations
- SW full-service median: $6,500; DW full-service median: $13,000; TW full-service median: $20,000
- Cited specific source platforms (ProMatcher, HomeAdvisor, HomeGuide, CostHelper, Quora firsthand reports) with dates and caveats about data freshness
- Gave detailed line-item breakdowns for disconnection ($250–$900 SW), running gear ($200–$800 SW), and per-mile transport rates ($4.00–$5.50/mi for toter truck)
- Long-distance pricing: SW 100-mi full-service $9,000–$16,000; DW $15,000–$24,000

### ChatGPT Summary
- Took the most conservative, source-grounded approach, anchoring primarily to ProMatcher's San Antonio-specific data points ($1,650 SW avg at ~50 mi, $3,000 DW avg at ~50 mi)
- Built a mathematical two-point linear model from ProMatcher's 50-mile and 500-mile anchors for distance interpolation
- Modeled single-wide full-service local mean at $5,333 with all-in realistic average of $6,213 -- the lowest among all platforms
- Provided the most detailed breakdown of TDHCA fee structures with specific TAC citations (10 TAC 80.3)
- Distinguished clearly between "hard-dollar" regulatory fees and market-driven variable costs

## Areas of Agreement
- All three platforms agree on core regulatory fees: TxDMV permit $40/section, TDHCA Form T $75 + $25/additional section, Statement of Ownership $55
- All agree transport-only is substantially cheaper than full-service, and that the base transport quote drastically understates true cost
- All cite the transport-only vs. full-service distinction as the primary source of consumer confusion in the market
- All identify permits, foundation/leveling, utility hookups, and skirting as major cost categories beyond the mover's invoice
- All agree double-wide moves roughly double transport costs due to two separate loads, two permits, and marriage-line complexity

## Areas of Divergence
- All-in cost estimates diverge significantly: Gemini's DW all-in of $19,400 is roughly 3x ChatGPT's modeled mean of ~$6,213 (SW); Gemini includes skirting, steps, and utility hookups in its all-in figure while ChatGPT's "all-in" adds only regulatory fees
- Claude's full-service DW range ($10,000–$16,000) sits between Gemini's ($7,000–$15,000) and Gemini's all-in ($19,400)
- Per-mile rate methodology differs: Gemini computed blended all-in per-mile rates ($311–$485), Claude cited transport-only per-mile ($4–$5.50), ChatGPT did not compute per-mile blended rates
- ChatGPT's ProMatcher-anchored transport-only figures ($1,300–$2,400 SW) are notably lower than Claude's ($1,500–$5,000) and Gemini's ($1,000–$3,500)
- Gemini provided the most detail on skirting costs (vinyl, insulated, faux brick, masonry) while the other platforms did not break this out

## Recommended Data for LocalMovers.com
