## Areas of Agreement
- All three platforms agree that single-wide "Most Common" full-service local pricing centers around $4,500-$6,500
- All agree San Antonio pricing sits below national averages (5-15% discount due to lower diesel, flat terrain, competitive market, cheap permits)
- All agree the "Most Common" tier captures 34-45% of moves -- the largest single bucket
- All agree distance increases total cost but decreases per-mile rate
- All agree winter months are cheapest and summer months are most expensive
- All identify setup, skirting, foundation, and utility hookups as the primary "hidden" cost categories beyond transport quotes
- All cite $40 TxDMV permit and $75 TDHCA Form T as baseline regulatory costs

## Areas of Divergence
- DW pricing diverges significantly: Gemini's Most Common is $7,500-$10,499; Claude's is $10,000-$14,000; ChatGPT's is $11,500-$14,500 -- Gemini's range appears to represent a transport+basic-setup scope while Claude/ChatGPT include more comprehensive full-service elements
- Budget tier floor: Gemini starts SW at $1,000; Claude at $1,000; ChatGPT at $2,200 -- ChatGPT's higher floor reflects its anchor to ProMatcher's SA-specific data
- Complex tier ceiling: Claude allocates 10% of SW moves to $8,000+; ChatGPT allocates 7% to $9,000+; Gemini allocates only 5% to $8,500+ -- different assumptions about how many moves are truly complex
- SA city installation permit: Claude says $98 (FY2026); Gemini implies it was absorbed into general building permit formulas; ChatGPT does not cite a specific city permit fee
- Only Gemini provided the Bexar County geotechnical narrative in the FAQ; only Claude covered SAWS impact fees and triple-wide distributions
