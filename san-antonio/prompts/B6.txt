Title: San Antonio Mobile Home Moving Hidden Costs & Surcharges — 2026 Market Analysis

For each hidden cost, provide: the cost item name, the dollar range, and the specific San Antonio-area cause/trigger. Cover at minimum:

1. Wind zone compliance: Texas Wind Zone II requirements for Bexar County — what does wind zone anchoring and tie-down add to setup cost? Is it mandatory for all manufactured homes in the San Antonio area? How do requirements differ from Wind Zone I areas further inland?

Hill Country terrain access: Costs for moves involving narrow, steep, or winding Hill Country roads (FM 1863, River Road, Galm Road, roads through Helotes, Boerne, Bulverde, Canyon Lake) — additional pilot cars, tree trimming, utility line lifts, low-water crossing restrictions, and road closure permits

3. Highway construction rerouting: When TxDOT restricts oversize loads on I-35 through downtown San Antonio (ongoing expansion project), Loop 410, or Loop 1604 construction zones, how much does the longer alternate route add in mileage, escort time, and permits?

4. Utility coordination with CPS Energy (electric and gas) and SAWS (San Antonio Water System) — disconnect/reconnect fees, scheduling lead times, and any rush-service surcharges. Also cover septic system requirements for rural Bexar County and Hill Country placements where municipal sewer is unavailable

5. Foundation and anchoring: Costs specific to San Antonio soil conditions — expansive black clay (vertisol) prevalent throughout Bexar County requiring engineered foundations, caliche/limestone in the Hill Country requiring different anchor systems, and varying soil conditions across the Edwards Plateau

6. FEMA flood zone elevation: San Antonio-area manufactured homes in SFHA (Special Flood Hazard Areas) — San Antonio River corridor, Salado Creek, Leon Creek, Olmos Creek, Medina River — what elevation requirements add to placement cost? How do post-2018 flood map updates affect requirements?

7. Manufactured home park space rent impact: When moving within or between manufactured home parks, do parks charge transfer fees, buy-in fees, or "lot prep" charges? What are typical park-related costs in San Antonio?

8. Pre-1976 (pre-HUD code) surcharge: What percentage of San Antonio-area transporters refuse pre-1976 homes? What structural reinforcement is required and what does it cost?

9. Transit insurance gap: Standard homeowner's policies don't cover transport — what does standalone transit insurance cost for San Antonio-area moves?

10. TCEQ (Texas Commission on Environmental Quality) compliance: Do any Texas environmental regulations affect the transport vehicles or the home placement (asbestos abatement for older homes, on-site sewage facility permits for rural placements)?

11. Tree trimming and power line lifts: Costs for CPS Energy line lifts — how many crossings are typical on a San Antonio-area route and what does each cost? Are heritage tree ordinances in San Antonio a factor?

12. Edwards Aquifer compliance: For placements over the Edwards Aquifer Recharge Zone, what additional environmental requirements (impervious cover limits, water quality controls) add to manufactured home placement costs?
